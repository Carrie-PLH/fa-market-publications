## What changed in the tooling today

All recorded in PROVENANCE.md.

- AMS 3649's reporting month is taken from the block's midpoint, not its end date. The September block had been labelled 2026-10; retained captures re-parsed, superseded rows flagged rather than edited.
- `issue.py` reads accumulated history for sources declared `accumulates` in sources.json. Without it, a source stating one period per report could never carry any comparison. Cheddar reported one week in record while the record held five.
- One rounding rule, `common.round_half_up`, shared by the formatter and the figure check. They must match or an edition prints a figure the gate will not find.
- `evidence.json` covers the captures behind accumulated readings, each naming its run and role. `site.py` publishes those under their own run id.
- `lobster/observations/tools/parse.py` fails a source rather than the whole pass. A captcha page had ended the 2026-10-01 pass and cost six of eight sellers.
- `publish.sh` preserves `site/.well-known/security.txt` across its rebuild. It had been destroying it.
- `check.py` reads only figures carrying a unit in prose. Bare counts and quantities written in words pass unexamined — check those by hand.
