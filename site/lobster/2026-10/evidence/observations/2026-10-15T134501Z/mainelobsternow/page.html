Live Lobster

Choose wild-caught live lobster for a lobster bake, celebration or dinner at home. Our 1–3 lb. Maine lobsters are sustainably sourced from our home fishery; larger 4–20 lb. options are wild-caught Canadian lobster. Check the selected listing for its origin, size and delivery options.

Maine’s lobster fishery is managed for sustainable harvest, with size limits, protections for egg-bearing and V-notched females, and trap rules that support the future of the fishery. Learn about our approach to sustainable seafood.

Plan your delivery, storage and cooking with our Maine lobster guide.

Our live Maine lobsters are traceable to our partner docks in Maine.

Sustainably Sourced Maine Lobster

Our 1–3 lb. Maine live lobsters are sustainably sourced and wild-caught in Maine. Live Lobster FAQ

Everything you need to know about ordering fresh Maine lobster online.
What size live lobster should I order?

Most customers choose 1.25–1.5 lb lobsters for a classic single serving. Larger lobsters (2–3 lbs) are great for sharing or special occasions.
For oversized whole lobsters, compare our jumbo live lobsters and check the selected size’s availability and ordering instructions.
Do all your live lobsters come from Maine?

Our 1–3 lb. Maine live lobsters come from Maine. The larger 4–20 lb. options are wild-caught Canadian lobster. Orders ship from Maine; check each listing for its harvest origin and available delivery dates.
How are live lobsters packaged for delivery?

Live lobsters are carefully packed in insulated containers with cold packs to maintain proper temperature and ensure they arrive alive and fresh.
How long can live lobsters survive after delivery?

When stored properly in the refrigerator, live lobsters can typically survive 24–36 hours after arrival.
Can I cook live lobster the same day it arrives?

Yes, we recommend cooking your live lobster the same day it arrives for the best flavor and texture. The best way to cook live lobster depends on lobster size and your equipment; compare the methods in our guide before you begin.
